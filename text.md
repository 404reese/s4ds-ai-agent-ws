### Why this is useful for your demo

The resume **actually matches the JD**, but it's poorly optimized. Your agent can demonstrate improvements such as:

* Move **Python + Django + PostgreSQL + AWS + Docker + REST APIs** to the foreground.
* Rewrite vague bullets like *“Worked on web applications”* into impact-oriented bullets.
* Emphasize **backend** rather than React/Node.js.
* Add relevant JD keywords such as **microservices, CI/CD, unit testing, production debugging** where supported by the candidate's experience.
* De-emphasize unrelated frontend skills.
* Strengthen the summary specifically for the role.
* Reorder skills according to JD relevance.
* Improve bullet structure and consistency.
* Avoid inventing experience or metrics.

That gives you a nice **“raw resume → JD-tailored resume”** comparison without making the original resume unrealistically bad.
